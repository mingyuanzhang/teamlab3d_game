A little curiosity goes a long way.

SIX SHAPES. A FEW SECRETS.

Make room for wonder.

Choose a shape, then tap the stage. Give it a moment: each one has a different 3D discovery. You have six of each.

Use the arrow to move a shape. Drag empty space to orbit, or use the rotation buttons. Scroll to zoom.

Draw paths and springs across the stage. Raise the building height to make elevated paths. Draw a ladder from its lower end to a raised path; it rises to the selected height.

Little people give each other space, wait on ladders, and find their own way. Shapes cannot be placed on them.

Choose Default, Jazz, Rock, Classical, Bach, or Chopin from the Music menu. Every discovery joins your chosen theme, following its chords and beat. Use the speaker button or M to mute; pause also pauses the music.
